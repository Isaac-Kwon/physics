The Standard Model is the theory of all known elementary particles and of three of the four forces between them. Gravity is not included.

**Matter particles** (spin ½) come in three families:

- quarks: up, down, charm, strange, top, bottom;
- charged leptons: electron, muon, tau;
- neutrinos: one for each charged lepton.

Ordinary matter uses only the first family: up and down quarks and the electron.

**Force carriers** (spin 1) are the photon (electromagnetism), the W and Z bosons (weak force) and eight gluons (strong force).

The **Higgs boson**, discovered in 2012, reflects the field that gives the W, Z and the matter particles their masses.

The model predicts experiments with remarkable precision. It cannot explain dark matter, neutrino masses or why matter dominates over antimatter, so we know it is incomplete.
