A **time projection chamber** (TPC) is a large, empty-looking volume filled with gas or liquid, with an electric field running along it.

1. A charged particle crosses the volume and ionizes the medium along its path.
2. The electric field pushes the freed electrons toward a readout plane at one end, at a constant drift speed.
3. The plane records **where** each electron arrives: two coordinates.
4. The **arrival time** gives the third coordinate, because time equals drift distance divided by speed.

The result is a full three-dimensional picture of the track. The amount of charge along the track measures the energy loss dE/dx.

The price: drifting takes time, so a TPC is slower than a silicon detector.
