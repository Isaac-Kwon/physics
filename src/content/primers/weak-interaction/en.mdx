The **weak interaction** is the force behind beta decay and the fusion that powers the Sun. It is carried by three heavy particles: the charged **W** bosons and the neutral **Z** boson, each about 90 times heavier than a proton.

Heavy carriers mean a very short range, about $10^{-18}$ m, a thousandth of a proton's size. A particle must come that close to feel the force, so at low energy the interaction is feeble and rare.

Neutrinos have no electric or colour charge, so the weak force is the only one that acts on them. That is why they pass through a planet.

Two kinds of event exist. Charged current: a W is exchanged and the particle changes identity. Neutral current: a Z is exchanged and the particle stays itself.

One oddity: the W couples only to left-handed particles. This breaks mirror symmetry (parity violation).
